ADB Manager 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 9 月 28 日

ADB Manager 不收集、存储或向开发者及第三方传输个人数据。

设备信息、ADB 操作记录、用户选择的文件和应用设置仅在用户的 Mac 与所连接的 Android 设备之间处理。无线调试会在用户的本地网络中与其指定的设备通信。

如有隐私问题，请联系 97413240@qq.com。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

ADB Manager does not collect, retain, or transmit personal data to the developer or third parties.

Device information, ADB operation history, user-selected files, and app settings are processed only between the user's Mac and connected Android devices. Wireless debugging communicates with devices selected by the user on the local network.

For privacy questions, contact 97413240@qq.com.
